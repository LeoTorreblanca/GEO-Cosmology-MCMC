# Limitations and Scope

This repository reports a numerical test of a specific cosmological
realization of the GEO framework.

## Convergence

The longest GEO run reached a final recorded R-1 of approximately
0.01731.

The stricter pre-specified target of R-1 < 0.01 was not reached before
the sample cap.

The exact diagnostic is therefore reported rather than describing the
chains as formally converged under the strict criterion.

## eta = 3/5

The profile-likelihood and cross-configuration analyses show that the
canonical eta = 3/5 node is numerically compatible with the tested
data and configurations.

These results do not independently establish eta = 3/5 as a universal
constant of nature.

Several cross-configuration tests reuse common cosmological
information and must not be counted as statistically independent
confirmations.

## Hubble-channel realization

The final cosmological test uses the realization

mu_H = eta

together with

R = mu_H^(1/3).

The validity and universality of this channel identification remain a
physical hypothesis of the GEO framework and should be tested with
additional independent observables.

## Model comparison

The reported Delta chi-square values refer to the exact matched
likelihood configuration supplied in this repository.

They should not be interpreted as a general Bayesian evidence ratio
or as proof that GEO supersedes LCDM.

## Local H0 likelihood

The provenance and exact observational reference corresponding to the
adopted local-H0 likelihood must be documented explicitly in any
submitted manuscript.

## Future tests

Important independent extensions include:

- alternative local-H0 likelihoods;
- BAO and supernova-only tests;
- weak-lensing and growth-only tests;
- out-of-sample predictions;
- Bayesian model evidence;
- independent replication of the full pipeline.
